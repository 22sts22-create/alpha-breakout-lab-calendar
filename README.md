FDA PDUFA Catalyst Calendar

A browser-based calendar that tracks and ranks upcoming FDA PDUFA dates — the regulatory decision deadlines that act as major catalysts for biotech and pharmaceutical stocks.

What it is

A focused tool for traders who follow biotech catalysts. FDA PDUFA dates (the deadlines by which the FDA must decide on a drug application) frequently trigger large moves in the underlying stock, and this calendar surfaces the upcoming ones in one place, ranked by significance.

What it does
Catalyst calendar — upcoming FDA PDUFA dates organized so the next catalysts are easy to see.
Ranking — catalysts are ranked so the most significant upcoming events stand out rather than being buried in a flat list.
Watchlist — track the specific names and catalysts you care about.
Glossary — plain-language definitions of the biotech/regulatory terms involved, so the calendar is usable by traders who aren't FDA experts.
Email alert capture — sign up to be notified about upcoming catalysts.
How it works

Built with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript, running in the browser. Presents catalyst data in a ranked, filterable calendar view, with a watchlist for tracking selected names and an email capture flow for alerts.

Why I built it

Biotech catalysts are some of the most tradeable events in the market, but the information is scattered and hard to prioritize. This pulls the upcoming FDA decision dates into one ranked view, with enough context (the glossary) that a trader doesn't need a pharma background to use it.

Note

This is an informational and educational tool. It surfaces publicly available regulatory calendar information and does not provide financial advice.
